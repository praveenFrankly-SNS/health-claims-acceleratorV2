Section 1.1
Policy Overview: The Gold Health Insurance Plan offers comprehensive health coverage designed for families and individuals seeking premium medical security. It covers advanced hospitalization expenses, modern treatments, and offers higher sum insured limits.

Section 1.2
Eligibility and Sum Insured: This policy is available for individuals aged 18 to 75. Base Sum Insured options range from $100,000 to $500,000. Coverage limits and sub-limits are calculated based on the chosen Base Sum Insured.

Section 2.1
In-Patient Hospitalization: We will cover medical expenses for hospitalization exceeding 24 consecutive hours. This includes room rent, nursing charges, intensive care unit (ICU) charges, specialist consultation fees, surgeon fees, operating theater charges, and cost of specialized medicines, implants, and drugs.

Section 2.2
Room Rent and ICU Limits: Under the Gold Plan, there is NO sub-limit on standard private room rent. The insured is eligible for a single private AC room. Intensive Care Unit (ICU) and High Dependency Unit (HDU) charges are fully covered up to the Base Sum Insured without daily limits. If a suite or deluxe room is chosen, a 15% co-payment will apply to the entire claim.

Section 3.1
Pre and Post Hospitalization: Medical expenses incurred up to 60 days prior to the date of admission, and up to 90 days post discharge, are fully covered under this policy, provided they are related to the same medical condition for which the hospitalization was approved.

Section 4.1
Coverage for Respiratory Illnesses: Treatment for acute and chronic lower respiratory infections, including but not limited to Bronchitis, Asthma, ARDS, and all forms of Pneumonia (including ICD codes J12.9 and J18.9), is comprehensively covered up to the Base Sum Insured with no mandatory co-payments.

Section 4.2
Cardiovascular Procedures: All cardiovascular treatments, including angioplasty, stent placement, bypass surgery, and pacemaker implantation, are fully covered up to the Base Sum Insured. Cardiac rehabilitation costs up to $2,500 post-surgery are also covered.

Section 4.3
Day Care and Modern Treatments: We cover medical expenses for all Day Care procedures which require less than 24 hours of hospitalization. Furthermore, modern treatments such as Robotic Surgeries, Oral Chemotherapy, and Stem Cell Therapy are covered up to 50% of the Base Sum Insured.

Section 5.1
General Exclusions: The policy does NOT cover any medical expenses incurred for:
1. Cosmetic, aesthetic, or plastic surgery, unless required due to an accident, burns, or cancer treatment.
2. Unproven or experimental treatments not recognized by standard medical bodies.
3. Rehabilitation therapies for substance abuse or alcoholism.

Section 5.2
Waiting Periods for Pre-Existing Diseases: Any disease, illness, or injury that was diagnosed or treated within 24 months prior to the policy inception is considered a Pre-Existing Disease (PED). Treatment for PEDs is covered only after 24 months of continuous coverage have elapsed.

Section 5.3
Specific Disease Waiting Periods: Treatment for the following conditions is excluded for the first 12 months of the policy:
1. Cataract and Glaucoma.
2. Hernia, Hydrocele, and Piles.
3. Joint replacement surgeries (unless due to an accident).

Section 6.1
Co-Payment: The Gold Plan operates on a zero co-payment basis for standard claims, regardless of the policyholder's age, provided treatment is taken in a standard single private room or below.

Section 7.1
Network Hospital Coverage and Cashless Processing: Claims arising from Network Hospitals are eligible for cashless processing. The insured must obtain pre-authorization at least 48 hours before planned admission.

Section 7.2
Non-Network Hospital Claims: Treatment taken at Non-Network hospitals will be processed on a reimbursement basis without any standard percentage deduction. The insured must submit original documents within 30 days of discharge.
