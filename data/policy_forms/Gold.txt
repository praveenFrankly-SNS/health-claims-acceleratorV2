Section 1.1
Policy Overview: The Star Gold Health Insurance Plan offers comprehensive health coverage designed for families and individuals seeking premium medical security. It covers advanced hospitalization expenses, modern treatments, and offers higher sum insured limits, along with a suite of wellness and restoration benefits intended to support policyholders through both planned and emergency care episodes.

Section 1.2
Eligibility and Sum Insured: This policy is available for individuals aged 18 to 75. Dependent children are eligible for cover from 91 days of age to 25 years under a Floater Sum Insured arrangement. Base Sum Insured options range from $100,000 to $500,000. Coverage limits and sub-limits are calculated based on the chosen Base Sum Insured. Family Floater cover may include Self, Spouse, Dependent Children, and Parents, subject to a maximum family size of 2 Adults and 4 Children per floater policy.

Section 1.3
Policy Term and Renewal: The Gold Plan may be purchased for a term of 1, 2, or 3 years. Lifetime renewability is guaranteed provided premiums are paid on time. A grace period of 30 days is allowed for renewal premium payment, during which continuity of waiting periods and accrued benefits is preserved, though coverage is suspended during the lapse window.

Section 2.1
In-Patient Hospitalization: We will cover medical expenses for hospitalization exceeding 24 consecutive hours. This includes room rent, nursing charges, intensive care unit (ICU) charges, specialist consultation fees, surgeon fees, operating theater charges, and cost of specialized medicines, implants, and drugs.

Section 2.2
Room Rent and ICU Limits: Under the Gold Plan, there is NO sub-limit on standard private room rent. The insured is eligible for a single private AC room. Intensive Care Unit (ICU) and High Dependency Unit (HDU) charges are fully covered up to the Base Sum Insured without daily limits. If a suite or deluxe room is chosen, a 15% co-payment will apply to the entire claim.

Section 2.3
Consumables and Non-Medical Items: Non-medical consumables incurred during hospitalization, such as gloves, syringes, PPE kits, and nebulization kits, are covered up to $1,500 per hospitalization as part of the base benefit, without requiring a separate add-on.

Section 2.4
Domiciliary Hospitalization: Medical treatment for a period exceeding 3 days, for an illness or injury which in the normal course would require care and treatment at a hospital but is actually taken at the insured's home because the patient is unable to be moved, or because of non-availability of a hospital bed, is covered up to the Base Sum Insured.

Section 3.1
Pre and Post Hospitalization: Medical expenses incurred up to 60 days prior to the date of admission, and up to 90 days post discharge, are fully covered under this policy, provided they are related to the same medical condition for which the hospitalization was approved.

Section 3.2
Ambulance Charges: Emergency road ambulance services are covered up to a limit of $2,000 per hospitalization, provided that the patient is transported to the nearest hospital with adequate medical facilities.

Section 3.3
Air Ambulance: Emergency air ambulance evacuation to the nearest hospital with adequate facilities, when advised by the treating medical practitioner and not available by road, is covered up to $10,000 per policy year.

Section 4.1
Coverage for Respiratory Illnesses: Treatment for acute and chronic lower respiratory infections, including but not limited to Bronchitis, Asthma, ARDS, and all forms of Pneumonia (including ICD codes J12.9 and J18.9), is comprehensively covered up to the Base Sum Insured with no mandatory co-payments.

Section 4.2
Cardiovascular Procedures: All cardiovascular treatments, including angioplasty, stent placement, bypass surgery, and pacemaker implantation (ICD code I25.10), are fully covered up to the Base Sum Insured. Cardiac rehabilitation costs up to $2,500 post-surgery are also covered.

Section 4.3
Day Care and Modern Treatments: We cover medical expenses for all Day Care procedures which require less than 24 hours of hospitalization. Furthermore, modern treatments such as Robotic Surgeries, Oral Chemotherapy, and Stem Cell Therapy are covered up to 50% of the Base Sum Insured.

Section 4.4
Cataract Treatment Sub-limits: Medical expenses for Cataract treatment (ICD code H25.9) shall be capped at a sub-limit of $60,000 per eye, or the actual expenses incurred, whichever is lower.

Section 4.5
Knee Joint Replacement Sub-limits: Expenses related to unilateral or bilateral knee joint replacement surgery (ICD code Z96.65) are capped at a sub-limit of $300,000 per surgery, unless arising directly from an accident.

Section 4.6
Maternity and Newborn Cover: Maternity expenses, including normal delivery, cesarean section, and pre/post-natal care, are covered as an optional add-on up to $15,000, subject to a waiting period of 24 months. Newborn baby coverage is activated from day one when the optional Maternity Cover is active and claims documentation includes submission of the 12-week and 20-week pregnancy scan reports.

Section 4.7
Organ Donor Expenses: Medical expenses incurred for the donor in connection with organ transplantation, including harvesting surgery, are covered up to $50,000, provided the recipient's claim for the transplant itself is admissible under this policy.

Section 4.8
AYUSH Treatment: In-patient treatment taken under Ayurveda, Yoga and Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, and Homeopathy systems of medicine at a recognized AYUSH hospital is covered up to the Base Sum Insured, subject to prior approval from the company for Yoga and Naturopathy treatment specifically.

Section 4.9
Annual Health Check-up: Policyholders are entitled to one annual health check-up package, including standard blood work and an ECG, at a Network Diagnostic Center, up to $200 per insured member per policy year, irrespective of claims made.

Section 5.1
General Exclusions: The policy does NOT cover any medical expenses incurred for:
1. Cosmetic, aesthetic, or plastic surgery, unless required due to an accident, burns, or cancer treatment.
2. Unproven or experimental treatments not recognized by standard medical bodies.
3. Rehabilitation therapies for substance abuse or alcoholism.
4. Dietary supplements and over-the-counter substances not prescribed as part of an active line of treatment.
5. Self-inflicted injury, unless resulting from a documented mental health crisis requiring inpatient psychiatric care.

Section 5.2
Waiting Periods for Pre-Existing Diseases: Any disease, illness, or injury that was diagnosed or treated within 24 months prior to the policy inception is considered a Pre-Existing Disease (PED). Treatment for PEDs is covered only after 24 months of continuous coverage have elapsed.

Section 5.3
Specific Disease Waiting Periods: Treatment for the following conditions is excluded for the first 12 months of the policy:
1. Cataract and Glaucoma.
2. Hernia, Hydrocele, and Piles (ICD code K40.90).
3. Joint replacement surgeries (unless due to an accident).
4. Calculus disease of the gall bladder, kidney, and urinary tract.

Section 5.4
Restoration of Sum Insured: If the Base Sum Insured is exhausted during the policy year due to claims, a one-time 100% restoration of the Sum Insured is triggered for unrelated illnesses. The restored amount cannot be carried forward to a subsequent policy year if unused.

Section 5.5
No Claim Bonus: For every claim-free policy year, the Base Sum Insured is increased by 10% at renewal, up to a maximum cumulative bonus of 50% of the original Base Sum Insured. The bonus is reduced proportionately, but not below zero, in any year in which a claim is made.

Section 6.1
Co-Payment: The Gold Plan operates on a zero co-payment basis for standard claims, regardless of the policyholder's age, provided treatment is taken in a standard single private room or below.

Section 6.2
Voluntary Deductible: Policyholders may opt for a voluntary deductible of up to $5,000 at policy inception in exchange for a premium discount of up to 15%. The deductible applies per claim and is borne entirely by the insured before the company's liability begins.

Section 7.1
Network Hospital Coverage and Cashless Processing: Claims arising from Network Hospitals are eligible for cashless processing. The insured must obtain pre-authorization at least 48 hours before planned admission. For emergency admissions, pre-authorization must be requested within 24 hours of admission.

Section 7.2
Non-Network Hospital Claims: Treatment taken at Non-Network hospitals will be processed on a reimbursement basis without any standard percentage deduction. The insured must submit original documents within 30 days of discharge.

Section 7.3
Claim Documentation Requirements: All reimbursement claims must include the original discharge summary, itemized hospital bills, original investigation reports, and the treating physician's prescriptions. Claims missing any of these documents may be returned for resubmission within 15 days.

Section 8.1
Tele-Consultation and Digital Services: Policyholders have access to unlimited audio, video, and text tele-consultations with empanelled general physicians through the insurer's mobile application, at no additional cost during the policy period.

Section 8.2
Wellness Program: Policyholders completing a defined set of annual health and fitness activities, tracked through the insurer's mobile app, are eligible for a renewal premium discount of up to 10%.

Section 9.1
Free Look Period: A free look period of 15 days from the date of receipt of the policy document is available, during which the policyholder may cancel the policy for a full refund, provided no claim has been made.

Section 9.2
Grievance Redressal: Policyholders may escalate unresolved grievances to the Grievance Redressal Officer at grievances@starinsuredemo.com, and subsequently to the Insurance Ombudsman if the matter remains unresolved after 30 days.
